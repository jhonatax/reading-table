Privacy

Without an account, everything you enter (your answers, your group, books you've read) stays in your own browser's storage on this device. Nothing is sent to us.

If you log in, we store your email address, your answers and recommendations, the readers you add to your group (the names and answers from their share codes), and the books you mark as read. This is stored with Supabase so you can get it back on any device. Only you can access your data.

We don't sell or share your data, use advertising, or run analytics trackers. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

You can delete your saved data at any time from the Account page. To also delete your login (email address), contact the site owner via the project's GitHub page.
